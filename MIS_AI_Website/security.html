Malware and Cyber Threats


The WannaCry Ransomware Attack

On May 12, 2017, a significant cyberattack known as WannaCry ransomware began spreading across computer systems worldwide. On comprimised PCs, this malware encrypted files and demanded money from victims in exchange for restoring access. Businesses, hospitals, and governmental agencies throughout several countries were impacted by the attack. Because it demonstrated how out-of-date software and unpatched vulnerabilities may expose enterprises to serious security threats, WannaCry became a significant event in cybersecurity.



Types of Malware

  1. Trojan Horse: Malicious software that disguises itself as a legitimate program to trick users into installing it.
  2. Ransomware: Malware that encrypts files or locks computer systems and demands payment to restore access.

Common Hacker Motives

  1. Information Theft: Hackers may attempt to access confidential business information, passwords, or personal records.
  2. Financial Gain: Hackers may steal banking information, demand ransom payments, or sell stolen personal data.

Prevention Strategies

  • Firewalls: Help monitor and control network traffic to reduce unauthorized access and cyber threats.
  • Multi-Factor Authentication: Adds another verification step, like text passwords or pre-set personal questions to help prevent unauthorized access to accounts.

Network Security

Network security helps organizations protect the information transmitted between computers and other devices. Monitoring network traffic can help identify suspicious activity and prevent unauthorized access.
